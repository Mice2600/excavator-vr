The problem

The first hours are the expensive ones.

  1. A beginner learns on a real machine: fuel, wear, and an instructor standing beside it.
  2. Mistakes cost: a tipped machine, a torn pipe, a person inside the swing radius.
  3. One machine teaches one trainee at a time, and only when the site and the weather allow.

The idea

Give every trainee a machine of their own, in VR.

Excavator VR puts a trainee in the cab of a working excavator, in virtual reality. The machine has real hydraulics and real weight; the ground can be dug anywhere, 50 metres deep; the dirt goes into a truck that hauls it away and tips it. Nothing is scripted and nothing can be damaged.

Who it is for

Vocational colleges, operator training centres, construction and mining companies that bring new operators onto tracked excavators, dump trucks and haulers.

Virtual reality

Put the headset on. You are in the cab.

  1. Quest 3

    Built for the standalone Meta Quest 3: no cable and no PC in the classroom. It also runs from a PC over Quest Link, and on a desktop screen for demonstrations.

  2. 72 fps

    Measured on the headset itself, in a digging stress test of an early build. Physics steps at the same 72 Hz as the display.

  3. 1 : 1

    Your eyes sit at the operator’s eye point. The cab is full size around you, with its monitor, levers and pedals where they are in the machine.

Virtual reality · Controls

Two ways to take the levers.

Smart mode

Hold the grip and move your hand: the bucket follows it. The triggers curl and open the bucket. Made so a first-timer can start digging straight away.

Classic mode

The ISO lever pattern of a real cab, on the controller sticks. The left hand swings and works the stick, the right hand lifts the boom and curls the bucket: the same hand movements as in the machine.

ExcavatorVR07 / 11

Multiplayer · Why together

Loading a truck is team work.

On a real site nobody digs alone. The excavator operator has to place the bucket over a body that someone else parked; the truck driver has to wait, pull away and come back. In Excavator VR a crew practises that together.

  • Two excavators can load one truck, or dig the same trench from both ends.
  • A late joiner receives only the ground that has changed, then the live site.
  • An instructor can take the fourth seat: watch from the sky, then drop into any free cab.
  • Each operator has a name and a colour; in Sky View they mark that person’s machine.
  • A seat that is taken cannot be taken twice. Leave the site and the seat is free again.

ExcavatorVR08 / 11

Feel · Ground

It pushes back.

  1. 34.3 MParelief pressure, 20 t class

    One pump, shared.

    Swing, boom, stick, bucket and both tracks draw on the same hydraulic pump. Ask for everything at once and everything slows down. That is the first habit a new operator has to learn.

  2. 8layers, each cutting differently

    The ground decides.

    Topsoil gives way. Compact gravel makes the arm strain. Press the boom down on rock and the machine lifts itself on its own arm.

  3. 0real damage

    Mistakes are allowed.

    Drive too close to the pit and the machine goes over the edge. Nobody is hurt and nothing is broken. Recover sets it upright, with the load still in the bucket.
