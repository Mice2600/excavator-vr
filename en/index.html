01VR

Put the headset on. You are in the cab.

No hall, no cab mock-up, no fuel. The training place is a chair, a headset and two controllers, and what the trainee sees is a 20-tonne machine at full size.

  1. Quest 3

    Built for the standalone Meta Quest 3: no cable and no PC in the classroom. It also runs from a PC over Quest Link, and on a desktop screen for demonstrations.

  2. 72 fps

    Measured on the headset itself, in a digging stress test of an early build. Physics steps at the same 72 Hz as the display.

  3. 1 : 1

    Your eyes sit at the operator’s eye point. The cab is full size around you, with its monitor, levers and pedals where they are in the machine.

02Feel

It pushes back.

A simulator only teaches if the machine behaves like one. This one was built physics first.

  1. 34.3 MParelief pressure, 20 t class

    One pump, shared.

    Swing, boom, stick, bucket and both tracks draw on the same hydraulic pump. Ask for everything at once and everything slows down. That is the first habit a new operator has to learn.

  2. 8layers, each cutting differently

    The ground decides.

    Topsoil gives way. Compact gravel makes the arm strain. Press the boom down on rock and the machine lifts itself on its own arm.

  3. 0real damage

    Mistakes are allowed.

    Drive too close to the pit and the machine goes over the edge. Nobody is hurt and nothing is broken. Recover sets it upright, with the load still in the bucket.

05Controls

Two ways to take the levers.

Smart mode

Hold the grip and move your hand: the bucket follows it. The triggers curl and open the bucket. Made so a first-timer can start digging straight away.

Classic mode

The ISO lever pattern of a real cab, on the controller sticks. The left hand swings and works the stick, the right hand lifts the boom and curls the bucket: the same hand movements as in the machine.

Try the ISO pattern

A flat sketch of the idea. Drag the two levers or use the keys: dig in front, swing to the truck, dump. The simulator does this in 3D, all around you.

06Cab

No floating menus. A real cab.

Both cabs are modelled part by part at full size: joysticks, travel levers, pedals, switch panels, gauges. Everything the operator needs to know is on the machine’s own instruments, where it would be on site.

Point at a number
  1. 1

    Left joystick

    Swing and stick, on the ISO pattern. Thumb buttons and a trigger on the grip.

  2. 2

    Right joystick

    Boom and bucket. Pull back to lift, tilt left to curl.

  3. 3

    Travel levers

    One for each track: forward, reverse, and a turn on the spot when they are opposed.

  4. 4

    Travel pedals

    The same two tracks under the feet, as in a real cab.

  5. 5

    Monitor

    Live: bucket fill, the level in the truck being loaded, engine speed and load, control mode. Refreshed five times a second.

  6. 6

    Engine speed dial

    On the right console, under the operator’s hand.

  7. 7

    Switch panels

    Lit switches on both armrest consoles.

  1. 1

    Steering wheel

    Turns as you steer. The steering is speed-sensitive and centres itself.

  2. 2

    Instrument cluster

    Speedometer, rev counter, four small gauges and a row of warning lamps, tilted towards the driver’s eye.

  3. 3

    Trip display

    Live: speed, gear, engine speed, the load in the body in per cent and tonnes, tipping angle.

  4. 4

    Parking brake valve

    The red valve beside the wheel. Leave the truck and its brakes go on.

  5. 5

    Gear lever

    Five forward gears. The simulator shifts them for you, so attention stays on the load.

  6. 6

    Pedals

    Accelerator and brake sit on the two controller triggers. Hold the brake at a standstill to reverse.

  7. 7

    Switches and heater

    Rocker switches, heater knobs and vents in the centre stack.

09Together

One site. Four operators.

One headset hosts and the others join over the same Wi-Fi. No internet, no server, no accounts.

  • Two excavators can load one truck, or dig the same trench from both ends.
  • A late joiner receives only the ground that has changed, then the live site.
  • An instructor can take the fourth seat: watch from the sky, then drop into any free cab.

In testingMultiplayer is built and verified between two copies on one PC. Headset-to-headset trials come next.
